The Ceremony

Sagai Venue

The ceremony will take place at Black Bears Farmstay, Jaipur, an open air garden space. Expect fresh air, soft grass, and a beautiful golden hour glow, with a hint of chill in the late evening.

Dress Code

Dress comfortably for an open air garden celebration, keeping the soft grass and chilly late evening breeze in mind. Wear whatever makes you feel your best, in any color except dark pink, and preferably not white, as these are reserved for the bride and groom. This is just a gentle preference, not a rule!

After Party

A small get-together for friends and those who enjoy a little music, a bonfire, and some fun photoshoots. Nothing formal or fancy, just a relaxed evening to hang out and continue the celebrations!
